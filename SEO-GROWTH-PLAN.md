# Rizon SEO Growth Plan

Updated: 2026-07-29

## Objective

Increase qualified organic enquiries for custom learning-platform work, not
traffic for its own sake. The programme should improve visibility for buyers
researching custom LMS development, migrations, integrations, compliance,
analytics, and alternatives to established platforms.

## Success measures

Track performance by landing page and locale:

- non-branded organic clicks and impressions;
- queries and pages moving from positions 4 to 20 into the top three;
- click-through rate for pages with meaningful impressions;
- number of indexable pages indexed by Google;
- organic visits reaching a relevant service or contact page;
- qualified enquiries and assisted conversions from organic landing pages;
- referring domains from relevant education, training, and software sites;
- Core Web Vitals pass rate.

Do not judge success by published article count alone.

## Priority 1: Repair crawl and internal-link defects

Status: completed on 2026-07-29

### Work

- Correct the broken relative `.md` links inside every localized version of
  the LMS reporting article.
- Crawl rendered production pages for 404s, redirect chains, broken assets,
  incorrect canonicals, and invalid hreflang targets.
- Add a repeatable link check to the release workflow.
- Check that every sitemap URL is indexable and returns its canonical URL.

### Completion criteria

- All internal article links resolve directly to a `200` page in the current
  language.
- No indexable page links to a 404.
- Hreflang targets are reciprocal, canonical, and return `200`.

### Completed

- Replaced all eight broken article links across English, French, Spanish,
  and German with direct localized blog routes.
- Confirmed no relative Markdown links remain in rendered MDX content.
- Confirmed the production build and all 290 generated pages pass.

The broader rendered-site crawl and automated release check remain useful
maintenance tasks when the production deployment is available.

## Priority 2: Establish a Search Console opportunity loop

Status: pending

### Work

- Verify Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools ownership.
- Submit the sitemap and review indexation after each meaningful release.
- Export query and page performance for the last 28 and 90 days.
- Create three working lists:
  - pages ranking in positions 4 to 20;
  - high-impression pages with weak click-through rates;
  - submitted pages that remain unindexed.
- Review these lists monthly and record each optimization with its date.

### Completion criteria

- Search Console is the source of truth for the next optimization sprint.
- Every priority page has a target query, current position, CTR, intended
  search intent, and next action.

4. LTI, SSO, SCORM, xAPI, and interoperability

### Work

### Completion criteria

- Each article belongs to one primary cluster.
- Each hub links to all relevant supporting content and a clear commercial
  next step.

## Priority 4: Optimize pages already close to ranking

Status: pending

### Work

Use Search Console rather than assumptions to choose pages. For each page in
positions 4 to 20:

- align the title and introduction with the query and search intent;
- answer missing follow-up questions clearly;
- add first-hand examples, screenshots, numbers, or implementation detail;
- improve headings, tables, summaries, and descriptive anchor text;
- strengthen links from relevant high-authority pages on the site;
- improve the CTA so it matches the reader's current problem;
- record the change and compare results after enough impressions accumulate.

### Completion criteria

- Optimizations are tied to observed queries and measured after release.
- Content changes add useful information rather than merely changing wording
  or publication dates.

## Priority 6: Create one link-worthy interactive asset

Status: pending

### Recommended first asset

A custom LMS cost and build-versus-buy calculator that lets a team model:

- current licences and revenue share;
- staff workaround hours;
- migration and integration costs;
- expected platform lifespan;
- maintenance and ownership costs;
- three-year total cost.

### Other candidates

- LMS migration readiness assessment
- GDPR or FERPA platform checklist
- LTI 1.3 integration test sheet
- LMS reporting metric dictionary

### Completion criteria

- The asset solves a real planning task without requiring contact details.
- It has a crawlable explanation page, useful default assumptions, and a
  shareable result.
- Relevant articles and service pages link to it.

## Priority 7: Publish stronger proof and case studies

Status: pending

### Work

Where client permission allows, add:

- initial constraints and why existing software did not fit;
- architecture and workflow decisions;
- learner, tenant, course, or migration scale;
- administrative hours removed;
- software fees or manual steps reduced;
- performance, completion, or operational improvements;
- screenshots, diagrams, and a clear description of Rizon's contribution.

If numbers are confidential, use ranges or explain the measurable operational
change without inventing precision.

### Completion criteria

- Each major service has relevant proof.
- Case studies contain concrete decisions and outcomes rather than only visual
  presentation.

## Priority 8: Earn relevant authority

Status: pending

### Work

- Contribute technical implementation articles to edtech and training
  publications.
- Participate in 1EdTech, Moodle, Canvas, accessibility, and learning
  technology communities where Rizon has genuine expertise.

  directories.
- Build partner pages or joint resources with complementary vendors and

directory submissions.

### Completion criteria

- Growth in relevant referring domains and links to service, hub, case-study,
  and tool pages.
- Links come from sites with topical relevance and real audiences.

Status: pending

### Work

- Tighten long titles while preserving the query and reader benefit.

- Keep structured data accurate and consistent with visible page content.
