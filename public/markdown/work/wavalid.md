# Wavalid

Real-time WhatsApp phone number validation for marketing and sales teams. Checks any number against WhatsApp in sub-100ms across 195+ countries, with batch uploads, a dashboard, REST API, and MCP server.

**Year:** 2026
**Discipline:** SaaS product development
**Stack:** Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL

![Wavalid landing page](https://rizon.agency/wavalid/landing-page.png)

## The problem

Teams doing cold outreach on WhatsApp were wasting sends on numbers that were never registered on the platform, hurting deliverability and wasting budget on list-cleaning tools that only checked format, not actual WhatsApp presence.

## Our solution

Rizon built a validation service that checks any phone number against WhatsApp in real time, sub-100ms, across 195+ countries. Users validate numbers one at a time or in bulk batches, track results on a dashboard with historical charts, and integrate via REST API, MCP server, or Chrome extension. Credit-based pricing with no subscriptions, and numbers are never sold or retained.
